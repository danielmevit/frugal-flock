Add a source-only manual capacity readings foundation: `bridge/capacity.py` and the standalone `tools/capacity-readings.py` record and show manual shared-budget readings under the selected `PROJECT/coord/capacity/readings.json`. They make no provider, model, network or auth request. Only fresh, fully validated readings expose a usable remaining value. Missing, invalid, stale, expired and future readings stay Unknown or non-usable, and a passed reset is not treated as refill. The correction addresses four defects the lead reproduced in the inherited candidate. The CLI now runs without `PYTHONPATH` from any directory. Invalid, oversized or unsupported state is refused byte-for-byte rather than overwritten. Every loaded record is strictly validated, so forged provider/boolean/9999 values become invalid state. Symlinks at the project, `coord`, `capacity`, state and lock steps are refused, with bounded reads and flock-guarded atomic replacement. Not part of release 0.5.5.
