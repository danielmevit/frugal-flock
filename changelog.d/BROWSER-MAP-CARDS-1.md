### Changed

- Work-map cards give one quarter to a local monochrome AI logo and agent
  name, and three quarters to the task title and recorded time. Worker groups
  show their current/latest observed task with a count subtitle.
- Inactive cards are muted while their red, green or yellow right-edge outcome
  strips retain a clearer colored tone. Focus, hover and selection restore full
  contrast. This presentation preserves the distinction between ended work
  and fully checked, reviewed, fresh evidence.
- Brand paths are embedded locally with their MIT notice; no external asset
  requests, new dependencies or browser permission changes are needed.
