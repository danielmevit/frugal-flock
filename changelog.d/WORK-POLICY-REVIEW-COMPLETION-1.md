# WORK-POLICY-REVIEW-COMPLETION-1

A live reviewer keeps its budget slot until that review process finishes. Another workflow in the same group is refused before its provider starts. Run and review wait in the shell, so a signal stops the recorded provider and reaps the slot holder instead of leaving both blocked. The guard records the sessions it starts and cleans those processes up when a check fails. The installer tagline is "Give your AI subscriptions a group project."
