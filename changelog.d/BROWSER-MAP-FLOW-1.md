### Changed

- Read the work map from left to right: project, worker, task, with compact
  ownership lanes and connections that avoid task cards.
- Running tasks appear first, with a strong neutral outline and `RUNNING NOW`
  label. An activity summary explains when no recorded workers are running.
- Finished history is muted until hovered, focused or selected. Only fresh,
  passed and approved work counts as finished; incomplete evidence stays visible.
- Project and worker grouping nodes show current activity rather than turning
  red for historical task failures. Accessible descriptions and details retain
  failure counts; individual task status strips keep their recorded evidence.
