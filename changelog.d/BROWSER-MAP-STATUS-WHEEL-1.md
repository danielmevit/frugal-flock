### Added

- Thin right-edge map status strips: green for finished checks/review, red for
  recorded failures, yellow for attention, and gray for active/missing evidence.
  A textual legend and accessible summaries preserve the meaning without color.

### Changed

- Wheel over the map now zooms at the pointer. Hold the middle mouse button
  and drag to pan horizontally or vertically, with browser auto-scroll suppressed.
  Left-button/touch drag, keyboard controls and page scrolling outside remain.
